  • Do not publish the thesis PDF, participant/session videos, consent sheets, ethics documents, recruitment materials, reviews, or response drafts without explicit approval.
  • Keep page claims soft: the visuals can support “helped users inspect model behaviour” or “made interaction more visible”, not stronger proof claims.
